The options

Both are big-mountain trips by Northeast standards. They're different kinds of weekends, though.

Option A · Jay, Vermont

Jay Peak

The snowiest mountain in the East, tree skiing everywhere, and an indoor waterpark for when your legs are done.

Cost

$737 per person

for lodging and a two day lift ticket. Assuming 13 people.

Ski rentals and transportation from NYC is extra!!

Jay Peak forces you to buy a package that includes lodging + lift tickets. Keisha, if you want to join and not ski, you can get a discount ;)

Why go

  • Known for deep snow and a boundary-to-boundary glade policy
  • Vermont's only aerial tram, straight to the 3,968′ summit
  • Everything's at the base: slopeside hotels, the Pump House indoor waterpark, an ice arena. A true, self-contained resort.

Trade-offs

  • Long drive... plan for at least 6 hours from NYC
  • Après and dining are mostly on-resort
  • Can be cold and windy up top in February

Where we'd stay

Village 4-Bedroom Townhouse
Managed by Jay Peak · South Village
  • Ski-in / ski-out, on the Grammy Jay and Raccoon Run trails
  • 4 bedrooms; the resort's 4BR Village units, technically sleeps 10, but I bet we could stretch it ;) Overflow would in a supplementary condo or hotel rooom on the resort grounds.
  • Full kitchen, multiple living areas, private ski locker
  • Overflow: an extra hotel room on the resort if we need more beds.

Option B · Lake Placid, New York

Lake Placid / Whiteface

Biggest vertical in the East, plus a real Olympic town with a walkable main street and lots to do off the mountain.

Cost

$501 per person

for lodging and a two day lift ticket. Assuming 13 people.

Ski rentals and transportation from NYC is extra!!

Breakdown = $242pp for lodging + $259 for two day lift ticket. This math is based off of 13 people.

Why go

  • Longest, steepest-feeling runs in the Northeast
  • Lake Placid is a proper town: restaurants, bars, shops on Main Street
  • Non-ski fun: bobsled rides at Mt. Van Hoevenberg, the toboggan chute onto Mirror Lake, skating on the Olympic Oval, the Olympic Museum
  • Great for anyone in the group who doesn't ski much

Trade-offs

  • Not ski-in/ski-out: a short drive to the mountain each day, and ~20 min into Lake Placid for dinners out
  • Exposed summit can be icy and windy

Where we'd stay

Serene, HotTub, Near Whiteface/Lake Placid, Arcade
Airbnb · Wilmington, NY · ★ 4.96 (98 reviews)
  • 9 beds across 3 levels, 3 baths; sleeps 12 with some doubling up (potentially one overflow needed for 13)
  • Hot tub, wood burning fireplace, arcade with 400+ games, outdoor fire pit & BBQ grill
  • Cathedral ceiling with wall of windows, nestled in Adirondack forest
  • Minutes from Whiteface ski area, ~20 min drive to Lake Placid village
